How it works

Three steps, no surprises

  1. Drop it in

    No appointment needed for small jobs. For a full service, book a slot so we can have parts ready.

  2. Agree the quote

    We check the bike over with you and text a fixed price before any work starts.

  3. Ride it away

    We text when it is ready. Every job gets a test ride and a written note of what was done.

Tyre pressure guide

Starting pressures for a 75 kg rider on dry roads. Heavier riders add about 1 psi per 3 kg. Never go above the limit printed on the sidewall.

Tyre width Used for Front Rear Pressure
25 mm Road racing 81 psi 88 psi
28 mm Road 66 psi 72 psi
32 mm Endurance road 52 psi 56 psi
38 mm Commuter 38 psi 41 psi
45 mm Gravel 29 psi 31 psi
57 mm Trail (2.25 in) 18 psi 20 psi
